# Specs

Summaries of the capabilities that are already specified and implemented. The full requirement and scenario text is in `openspec/specs/<capability>/spec.md`, and that file is the authority when a summary here disagrees with it. app-shell, app-theming and app-platform came from the change `basic-app-layout` (`openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-01-basic-app-layout/`). item-creation, item-storage and timeline-feed came from `add-text-items` (`openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-01-add-text-items/`), which also changed app-shell and app-theming. item-editing came from `edit-text-items` (`openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-02-edit-text-items/`), which also changed item-storage, timeline-feed and app-theming. adaptive-layout came from the change of the same name (`openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-02-adaptive-layout/`), which also changed app-platform, app-shell, timeline-feed, item-creation and item-editing. `add-image-items` (`openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-02-add-image-items/`) and `event-items` (`openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-06-event-items/`) each changed item-creation, item-editing, item-storage, timeline-feed, adaptive-layout and app-theming.

* [app-shell](app-shell.md) - The frame around every main screen: header, title field, tab bar, create button and its per-section action, scrolling and touch targets.
* [app-theming](app-theming.md) - Light and dark palettes, contrast guarantees, live scheme switching, status bar and backgrounds.
* [adaptive-layout](adaptive-layout.md) - Four layouts from the window size (phone or tablet, portrait or landscape), size sets, frames, the content column, safe areas and rotation keeping the user's place.
* [app-platform](app-platform.md) - Android and iOS phones and tablets in any orientation, Expo Go, native splash, and no starter leftovers.
* [item-creation](item-creation.md) - The creation box: choose a type, write a text, pick a photo, or pick photos for an event with a title, category and description, Crear, Cancelar, back button and drafts across tabs.
* [item-editing](item-editing.md) - The edit dialog: change a text, image or event item with Guardar, close it without changes, or delete it after a confirmation.
* [item-storage](item-storage.md) - Local SQLite storage: item record, UUIDs, timestamps, soft deletion into a history, editing, text, image and event limits, ordered event photos, photo files, atomic saves and migrations.
* [timeline-feed](timeline-feed.md) - The Timeline: items newest first, the text, image and event cards and their actions, edits and deletions shown at once, and the empty, loading and error states.

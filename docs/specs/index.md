# Specs

Summaries of the capabilities that are already specified and implemented. The full requirement and scenario text is in `openspec/specs/<capability>/spec.md`, and that file is the authority when a summary here disagrees with it. All three came from the change `basic-app-layout`.

* [app-shell](app-shell.md) - The frame around every main screen: header, title field, footer tabs, create button, scrolling and touch targets.
* [app-theming](app-theming.md) - Light and dark palettes, contrast guarantees, live scheme switching, status bar and backgrounds.
* [app-platform](app-platform.md) - Android and iOS in portrait, Expo Go, native splash, and no starter leftovers.
